Design note for Finn (build from this)
  • Route: /fulfillment (the existing Overview). Fulfillment role; admin gets the same page for the whole team (copy swaps "your" for "the team's"). Phone layout keeps the Today fold (usePhoneLayout).
  • Layout: v16. Hero (46px greeting, 32px on phones, count clock 52px / 40px), then four job tiles (4 across, 2 across on phones), then What to do next (left, 1.5fr) and My pay (right, 1fr) from 1100px; stacked below that. Max width 1240px.
  • The four jobs: Live calls waiting, Callbacks due, No-shows to rebook, Servicing due. One count function returns all four plus the next list (Fulfillment Build Order, Lane A item 7). Each tile links to its desk: Leads desk (Live calls tab), Leads desk (Callback), Leads desk (Appointments, no-show filter), Servicing. Live calls shows 0 and the line "Live calls are switched off for now" while voice_live is off; the tile never flips the switch.
  • Colour: tokens only. Amber (.ov-attn) only on a tile with something due and the "Callback" / "Lapse save" chips. Green only on the caught-up tick. Selected or link accent is --ov-data-a.
  • My pay: money in JetBrains Mono (.money), big figure 40px / 34px on phones. Test values only; rates are read from settings and are Brayden's. The "Payouts are off" line shows while payouts_live is off. No pay line for appointments, transfers or servicing until rates are set.
  • What to do next: top 5 by due time across the four jobs, then "See the other N". Rows are 64px+ links to the item on its desk. Long client names wrap.
  • Nothing goes out: no button here sends a text, email or call.
  • Phones (390): every control is 44px or taller, tiles in 2 columns, nothing scrolls sideways.
  • Mockup only: names, carriers and amounts are made-up samples. Fonts fall back to system fonts and the backdrop dot grid isn't drawn; the real app gets both from DashboardLayout. The phone and desktop layouts come from the same page: open it at 390 and at 1440 to see each.
