Design note for Finn (build from this)
  • Route and nav: /fulfillment/servicing, fulfillment role (admin only if the existing guard lets admin in). Nav item "Servicing" next to the Leads desk.
  • Layout: same shape as the approved VA desk. From 1100px: queue left (400px), the step you hold on the right. Below that it's one column, and while you hold a step the queue is hidden (Release or an outcome brings it back).
  • Queue: count tiles (Overdue, Due today, Requests), filter chips by task kind (44px, wrap, counts), then Due now (the only amber card, including the due lines), Agent requests, Coming up. Sorted by due date. Rows are 64px+; long client names wrap.
  • Claim: tap a row, it holds the step for 15 minutes, like va_claim. A lost race shows the neutral line and refetches. One step at a time.
  • Held view: .ov-hero with Release, step and client name, chips, hold clock, a tel: link (no dialer), and the logistics line. Then the steps for that task type, then the outcome card.
  • Steps by type: packet check, review call, payment check month n of 12, lapse save, agent request (requested, scheduled, done). The "Steps for each task type" section shows each one. Outcome choices change per type; the layout does not.
  • Outcome: pick, write a note (the agent sees it on the client timeline), Mark done. Send to agent is the only route for a coverage question: it opens a needs-attention item for the agent. Cancel a step needs a reason. All through functions (claim, record outcome, done, send to agent), never a direct status write.
  • Payment check: "Missed" opens a lapse save due in 1 business day and warns the agent before any clawback. Shown as a neutral line, not red.
  • Nothing goes out: no button sends a text, email or call. sms_live and voice_live untouched.
  • Not on this screen: quotes, premiums, coverage or benefit wording, advice. An unlicensed VA never tells a caller to drop a policy.
  • Colours: tokens only. Selected = --ov-data-a / -tint; ticks = --ov-data-b; amber only in Due now; errors = --danger. The dashed box marked legal text: door holds consent wording, which isn't drafted here.
  • Phones (390): every control is 44px or taller, outcome cards stack, buttons go full width, no sideways scroll.
  • Mockup only: names, carriers and dates are made-up samples. Fonts fall back to system fonts and the backdrop dot grid isn't drawn; the real app gets both from DashboardLayout. The phone and desktop layouts come from the same page: open it at 390 and at 1440.
