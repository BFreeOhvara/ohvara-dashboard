Design note for Finn (build from this)
  • Route and nav: /fulfillment/leads, same as the approved VA desk. The page gains a two-tab switch under the title: Live calls and Appointments. Tabs are 44px, count in a small pill. Appointments is the default.
  • Appointments tab: the approved VA desk unchanged (queue left 400px from 1100px, lead held on the right, queue hidden on phones while holding, 15-minute claim via va_claim, 4-item checklist, Book / Record a transfer / Not reached), plus one new queue section, No-shows to rebook (count tile and card). A no-show is rebooked inside the hour, twice, then it falls back to Not reached. Rebook uses the same day and slot picker and keeps the same agent, inside that agent's working hours.
  • Tabs while holding: the other tab is disabled until you finish or release.
  • Live calls tab: built behind voice_live. While it's off the tab shows the "switched off" card and a link to Appointments; the count is 0. When on: a Calls waiting card (amber while something rings) with Answer, then the same checklist, then Transfer, Book or Drop. The switch is never flipped from here.
  • Nothing goes out: no button sends a text, email or call; sms_live and voice_live untouched. The phone number is a tel: link.
  • Colours: tokens only. Amber only on Callback due, the rebook "Rebook by" lines (inside the page's normal cards, never a new amber card) and a ringing call. Selected = --ov-data-a; ticks = --ov-data-b; errors = --danger. The dashed legal text: door box holds consent wording, which isn't drafted here.
  • Not on this screen: quotes, prices, coverage or benefit wording, advice.
  • Open question for Atlas: the approved desk unlocks outcomes at 4 of 4 including "Not reached". Same recommendation as before: unlock "Not reached" at any time. Mocked as approved.
  • Phones (390): every control is 44px or taller, tabs split the width evenly, buttons go full width, slots 3 across, nothing scrolls sideways.
  • Mockup only: names, lead types and times are made-up samples. Fonts fall back to system fonts and the backdrop dot grid isn't drawn; the real app gets both from DashboardLayout. The phone and desktop layouts come from the same page: open it at 390 and at 1440.
